About QuranToSoul

A free Quran app with no ads, for understanding the Quran word by word: the meaning under every word, the grammar behind it, its root, and recitation with each word highlighted. Made sabeel lillah.

Contact and corrections: qurantosoulapp@gmail.com · Privacy policy · Terms of Service

Please read: this is not a fatwa

The Arabic text of the Quran in this app is taken unchanged from the sources below and was checked letter by letter against the King Fahd Complex 1441H Madinah Mushaf.

The word meanings, grammar notes, "why this meaning" notes and ruku summaries are study aids. Parts of them were written or put together for this app and are drafts until reviewed by qualified scholars. They are not a translation of the Quran and not a religious ruling. For any question of belief or practice, ask a qualified aalim.

If you find a mistake, use "Report a mistake" in the app or email us. Every report is read and corrected.
