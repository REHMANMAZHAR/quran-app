Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026 · applies to qurantosoul.com and the QuranToSoul Android app

In short: no sign-up, no ads, no tracking cookies. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (language, font, text size, reciter, theme).
  • Where you stopped reading, bookmarks, notes, highlights, reading progress and hifz practice results.
  • Quran text, translations and any audio you download for offline use.

This is kept in your browser's or app's storage on your phone. We cannot see it. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling deletes it.

What we receive

  • Report a mistake: if you send a report, we receive the place in the Quran, the word or text it is about, what you wrote, the app version and the time. Add your email only if you want a reply. We use reports only to correct the app.
  • Visit counts: we may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page visits without cookies and without identifying you.
  • Our host (Cloudflare) handles normal web requests, like any website, and keeps short technical logs for security.

We do not sell, share or use any information for advertising. There are no accounts.

Other services the app connects to

  • Recitation audio streams from everyayah.com, mp3quran.net and the Quran.com audio server.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts.
  • Videos (Dr. Israr Ahmed's Bayan-ul-Quran and others) play from YouTube only when you open one. YouTube's own privacy policy applies to that video.
  • Links to tanzeem.org, sunnah.com and quran.com open those websites.

These services see your device's internet address when they send you a file, as any website does.

Microphone (Hifz check only)

The microphone is used only after you start the Hifz check and allow it. Your phone's built-in speech recognition turns your recitation into text (in Chrome this is Google's service; on iPhone it is Apple's). QuranToSoul does not record, keep or upload your voice.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone.

Contact

Questions or requests: qurantosoulapp@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the date above will change.
