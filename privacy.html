Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 November 2026 · Applies to qurantosoul.com and the QuranToSoul app.

This policy explains what information QuranToSoul keeps on your device, what is sent to us when you choose to report a mistake, and what happens when the app connects to other services. QuranToSoul is currently offered without user registration and without advertising.

1. Information kept on your device

Depending on the features you use, your browser or app storage may contain settings (such as language, font, text size and theme), reading position, bookmarks, notes, highlights, reading progress and hifz practice results. Some Quran resources or audio may also be cached or saved for offline use. This locally stored information is not routinely sent to QuranToSoul by those features. Clearing site/app storage or uninstalling the app may remove it.

2. Mistake reports you choose to send

If you use “Report a mistake”, the report is sent to our server. The report may include the Quran reference, the relevant word, report category, the message you write, an email address if you choose to provide one, interface language, app version, submission time and the country code made available by Cloudflare. Please do not include sensitive personal information in a report.

Reports are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database so the project team can review and respond to them, correct reported issues and maintain a record of the report. Providing an email address is optional; it may be used to contact you about your report. Reports are not used for advertising and are not sold. Access is intended for people maintaining the service. We retain reports for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the report, maintain service integrity and meet applicable obligations.

3. Hosting, security logs and analytics

QuranToSoul is hosted using Cloudflare services. When you visit the website or use server features, Cloudflare and the hosting infrastructure may process technical information such as your IP address, browser/device information, request details and security events as part of delivering and protecting the service. Cloudflare may retain technical logs under its own policies and service settings.

Cloudflare Web Analytics may be used to understand aggregate site traffic. Analytics configuration can change; consult Cloudflare’s privacy information for details of its processing.

4. Third-party services and external links

Some features connect to services operated by others. Depending on what you open or use, these may include:

  • Recitation audio: audio streams or files from EveryAyah, mp3quran.net and Quran.com/Quran Foundation services.
  • Fonts: fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts.
  • Speech recognition: if you start the Hifz/microphone feature, your device/browser speech-recognition provider may process audio to return recognised text. Depending on your device and browser, this may involve Apple, Google or another provider. QuranToSoul does not intentionally store a recording of your voice through this feature.
  • Video: videos opened from YouTube are subject to YouTube’s own privacy practices.
  • External references: links may open Quran.com, Tanzil, Quranic Arabic Corpus, Tanzeem and other third-party websites.

Those providers may receive technical information, including your IP address, when your device requests their content. Their own privacy policies and terms apply. Opening an external link takes you outside QuranToSoul.

5. Accounts and children

QuranToSoul currently does not require you to create an account to use its core reading features. If account or Quran ID sign-in features are introduced, the applicable information and choices will be explained before those features are used. The app is designed as a general-audience Quran resource; parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of internet-connected devices and third-party services.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose not to submit a mistake report, leave the optional email field blank, disable microphone access through your device/browser settings, and avoid opening third-party links or media. You can clear locally stored app data through your browser or device settings. To ask about a report you submitted or make a privacy request, contact qurantosoulapp@gmail.com. We may need enough information to identify the relevant report before acting on a request.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, its service providers or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a new version is published.

8. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: qurantosoulapp@gmail.com.
